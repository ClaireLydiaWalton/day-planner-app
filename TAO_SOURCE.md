# Tao Te Ching source notes

The chapter card is bundled locally and makes no network requests.

James Legge's 1891 English translation is sourced from [Project Gutenberg Ebook #216](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/216). Project Gutenberg marks this ebook public domain in the United States.

`tao-chapters.js` preserves Project Gutenberg's prose paragraph divisions, numbered sections, and lineated verse blocks. No alternate translation, commentary, or Chinese text is bundled.
